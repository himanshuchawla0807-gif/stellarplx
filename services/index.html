What We Do

Editing in service
of the viewer.

Five parts of one job: take the episode you recorded and make it genuinely good to watch, then give its best moments a life of their own.

Service 01

Podcast & Interview Editing

Long conversations are the hardest thing to edit well. We keep the thinking and cut the drag: dead air, restarts, the tangent that never lands. What is left is the same conversation, just easier to stay inside.

Service 02

Long-Form YouTube Edits

We build the episode around the way people actually watch: an opening that earns the next minute, a middle that keeps its shape, and a close that pays off what you promised. Structure first, polish second.

Service 03

Short-Form Clipping

One episode holds several ideas worth watching on their own. We pull them out and edit each into a self-contained clip, framed for the platform it lives on, with enough context to make sense to a stranger.

Service 04

Captions, Sound & Graphics

Clean audio, readable captions, and on-screen support that appears when it helps and stays out of the way when it does not. None of it is there to be noticed. All of it is there to keep the watch smooth.

Service 05

Publish-Ready Delivery

Every episode comes back organised: the long-form master, the clips, the correct aspect ratios, named the way your workflow expects. You open the folder and publish, without a round of file admin first.
