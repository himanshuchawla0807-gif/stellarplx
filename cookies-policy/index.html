Cookies Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small tracking text files stored on your device that help web developers analyze interactions and save website preferences to improve functionality and performance.

2. Exactly How We Use Them

Stellarplx uses analytical cookies to measure site interactions (e.g., page views, length of engagement) and functional cookies to ensure a smooth browsing experience, such as keeping session states across routes.

3. Managing Preferences

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. Detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.
